The 5 deal types, in plain terms

▾
Full Buyout

You sell it, they own it, done.

Someone pays you once for the idea and everything you've made toward it. After that it's fully theirs — you don't get anything else later, even if it takes off, but you also never have to think about it again.

Royalty / Revenue Share

A small slice, every time it earns.

No payment now. Instead, every time it makes money, you automatically get a percentage of that — like a check that shows up whenever it earns, for as long as the deal runs.

Hybrid

Some cash now, plus a smaller slice later.

You get paid something today to make it worth handing off, and you also keep a smaller ongoing percentage in case it really takes off.

Milestone Earn-Out

Paid in stages, as it actually succeeds.

Instead of one number, you're paid in check-ins tied to real progress — some now, more once it hits real users or real revenue. Less all at once, but tied to what actually happens.
