How it works

Some of us love to start things but not finish them.
Some of us love to finish things but not start them.
And both are okay.

Espalier connects starters with finishers to bring incredible ideas to life that might otherwise just sit on the sidelines — freeing both to do what they actually do best. And both sides get to make real money from it.

If you're a starter

You've got no shortage of ideas — the hard part is ever finishing one. Here's exactly what happens when you list one here.

01

Tell it like it is

Describe it in your own words. Attach files, drop in a voice memo, add links — whatever shows the real thinking you've already put in.

02

Get scored, not judged

Espalier checks for one thing: a genuine, thought-out attempt. Not polish, not perfection — real effort.

03

Approved — or a second look

Most genuine ideas go live right away. If one's flagged, you'll see exactly why.

04

Live in the marketplace

Finishers looking for exactly this kind of thing can find it and reach out.

05

Sign a deal, keep a stake

Cash now, an ongoing share, or both — you're locked in for whatever it becomes.

If you're a finisher

Starting from zero is the part you dread — but handed a half-built idea with real bones, you're unstoppable. Here's how it works.

01

Browse real progress

Every listing already has real work behind it — not just a raw idea someone jotted down.

02

See exactly where it stands

A forecast score, what's built and what's not, what it'd cost to finish, and why.

03

Propose your terms

Cash upfront, an ongoing share, or a mix of both — whatever fits the work ahead.

04

Sign and take it over

Once terms are agreed, the idea — and everything behind it — is yours to run with.

05

Build it, launch it, keep the majority

Do the real work, launch it, grow it — and keep the lion's share.
