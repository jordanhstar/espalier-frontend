✓ What gets published here

Espalier checks every idea before it goes live — not for quality or polish, just for whether it's a genuine, thought-out attempt. Here's exactly what that means:

You'll need: a real title and one-line hook, a description with some real substance (or files that show it), at least one step that's actually solid or partial — not everything left at "open" — and a real sense of what it could be worth.
You won't need: a polished idea, a conventional one, or a finished one. Weird, ambitious, or rough ideas are exactly what this place is for — as long as there's something genuine behind it.
What gets turned away: ideas with no real work behind them yet, or ones that don't read as a serious, coherent attempt at anything real.
If yours gets denied: you'll see exactly why, and you can either fix it and resubmit, or ask a human to take a second look.
